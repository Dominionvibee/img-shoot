# Reference assets

Original public imagery, video, fonts and frontend vendor scripts were captured from [IMG Models](https://imgmodels.com/) on 20 November 2026 for the F01 reference implementation. The source-to-local URL mapping is recorded in `../data/asset-map.json`.

Photography and video URLs originate from `imgmodels.com/wp-content/uploads/` and `storage-us.mediaslide.com/imgmodels/pictures/`. Font files originate from the IMG Models theme build. Their original names and contents are retained.

React 18 and ReactDOM retain their original copyright and license headers. The accompanying `original/REACT-LICENSE.txt` is the [React v18.3.1 MIT license](https://github.com/facebook/react/blob/v18.3.1/LICENSE).

These source attributions identify the reference material; they do not transfer ownership of the IMG Models branding, photography, video or fonts.

The local scouting assets under `scouting/` originate from https://getscouted.imgmodels.com/ and its public MediaSlide videos. The legal fonts and logos under `legal/` originate from WME Fashion and its public Typekit font URLs. The privacy logo, fonts and SVG under `privacy/` originate from the linked OneTrust webform. They were captured on 20 November 2026 for the local F01 rendering; original ownership is retained.

Additional captures on 21 November 2026 retain the corporate IMG policy CSS/fonts/logos under `corporate/`, OneTrust component styles and public cookie metadata, and the original jQuery/WordPress MediaElement video-player files under `mediaelement/`. Their source license and copyright headers are retained. The local privacy form loads Google's actual reCAPTCHA service with its documented public v2 test key; no production domain key or verification backend is supplied.
